# Privacy Policy for Screenshot AutoCleaner **Effective date:** October 24, 2026 Screenshot AutoCleaner ("the App") helps users identify and manage screenshots stored on their devices. This Privacy Policy explains how the App accesses information, how information may be processed, and what choices users have. ## 1. Information Stored on Your Device Screenshot AutoCleaner accesses photos and screenshots on your device to identify screenshots and support cleanup features. Depending on the permissions granted and the features you use, the App may access your device's media library to identify, display, and manage screenshots. The App is designed to leave ordinary photos and videos untouched by screenshot-cleaning operations. Screenshot identification and cleanup settings are intended to operate locally on your device. The App does not currently use its own backend server to store your screenshot library. Users can manage the relevant media permissions through their device settings. ## 2. Photos and Media Permissions The App requests media permissions to identify and manage screenshots. The App uses these permissions to provide its screenshot-cleaning functionality. Users may decline or revoke permissions, although some features may then be unavailable. The App does not require users to upload their screenshot library to a Screenshot AutoCleaner server to use its local cleaning functionality. ## 3. Notifications The App may request permission to send notifications relating to screenshot cleanup, reminders, or other features. Users can allow or deny notification permissions and can change their notification settings through their device settings. Notifications are intended to help users manage their screenshots and use the App. ## 4. Advertising and Google AdMob The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interaction information, diagnostic information, and other information as described in Google's applicable privacy disclosures. This information may be used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes, subject to applicable settings and requirements. Advertising technologies may collect or process information even though Screenshot AutoCleaner does not currently operate its own backend server. Users may be presented with privacy and consent choices where required. Advertising and consent settings may affect the advertisements shown. For more information about Google's data practices, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy ## 5. Email, Account Verification, and Purchases Screenshot AutoCleaner is intended to support account verification and purchase-related features that may use a user's email address. These features are planned and may not yet be available in the current version of the App. When implemented, the App will explain what information is required for account verification and purchase management and how that information is processed. Email addresses may be used for account identification, verification, purchase-related support, and other purposes disclosed when the relevant features are introduced. Information processed by Google Play for purchases is also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy disclosures. ## 6. Data Sharing Screenshot AutoCleaner does not currently operate its own backend server for storing users' screenshot libraries. The App uses Google AdMob for advertising. Google and its relevant service providers may process information as necessary to provide advertising and related services. If account verification, purchase management, analytics, or other third-party services are introduced, this policy will be updated as necessary to describe the relevant information processing and sharing practices. ## 7. Data Security We aim to protect information processed by the App through appropriate technical and organizational measures. No application or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep their devices and operating systems updated and protect access to their devices. ## 8. Data Retention and Deletion Screenshot AutoCleaner is designed to perform screenshot identification and cleanup locally on the device. Screenshots deleted using the App may be subject to the device's media-management behavior, including any applicable confirmation or recovery options. Information processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion practices. When account verification or other online features become available, additional information about account data retention and deletion will be provided as appropriate. ## 9. Children's Privacy Screenshot AutoCleaner is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children in violation of applicable law. Advertising and other features must be configured appropriately for the intended audience and applicable legal requirements. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately may contact us using the details below. ## 10. You can also manage applicable advertising privacy choices through the consent options made available in the App. For requests concerning information processed by Google, you may need to contact Google or use the privacy controls provided by Google. ## 11.
